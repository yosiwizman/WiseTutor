WiseTutor runs on Mr W's ai-desktop computer at home.
This launcher just opens the app in your browser.

HOW TO INSTALL
--------------
Step 1: Unzip this folder. You should see WiseTutor.app.

Step 2: Drag WiseTutor.app into your Applications folder.

Step 3: Right-click WiseTutor.app -> Open
        (The first time, Mac may ask whether you trust it. Click Open.)

Step 4: After that, launch from Launchpad or Applications like any other app.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
If nothing happens, check two things:
  - Make sure ai-desktop is turned on at home.
  - Make sure you are connected to the home Wi-Fi network.

Alternate entry point: double-click WiseTutor.command in Finder.
That does the same thing without needing the Applications folder.
