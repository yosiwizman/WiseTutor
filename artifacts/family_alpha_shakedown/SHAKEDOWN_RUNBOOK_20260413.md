## Step 3 — Send a text message

**Do:** Type "Hello, who are you?" in the message box and press Send (or hit Enter).

## Step 4 — Voice turn (speak and hear reply)

**Do:** Click the small headphones button next to the mic icon, then say "what is two plus two" out loud when the app is listening.

**Look for:** The reply appears on screen AND is spoken aloud through the speakers.

**Pass if:** You see the reply text AND hear it spoken clearly through the speakers.
**Fail if:** Reply appears silently with no audio, or nothing happens at all.

## Step 5 — Cancel a voice turn before speaking

**Do:** Click the headphones button again to start a new voice turn, then click it a second time to cancel before you say anything.

**Look for:** The app stops listening cleanly and the headphones button returns to its normal resting state.

**Pass if:** Button goes back to normal, no spinner or frozen state.
**Fail if:** The button stays in an active state, or the app feels stuck.

## Step 7 — Mic permission denied or timeout
